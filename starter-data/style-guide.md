# Style guide

This file teaches Claude how you write — your voice, the patterns you reach for, the things you avoid. Claude consults this whenever it drafts a message, comm, or doc on your behalf.

This is a starter version. **It will only be useful once you fill it in with your own preferences.** Edit your drafts, tell Claude what to change, and ask it to update this file with the lesson learned. Over a few weeks of use, it'll match your voice closely.
